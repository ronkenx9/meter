How METER works

A card hold up front, a payment for every step of the work, and a charge for exactly what was spent. This page covers running it, the rules that keep the money safe, and how to check every claim yourself.

Overview

METER is an AI research service built for the Airwallex Agentic Banking Hackathon, Kit 12: Inference Sourcing Agent. Several customers each order a report. An agent buys every paragraph, search and lookup from paid providers, shares a short float of working money between the jobs, and never charges anyone past the hold they approved.

  • Airwallex holds each customer's card: a pre-authorization, raised only with the customer's yes, and captured once.
  • Metal pays the providers: each request is paid over x402 from a job's own clearing wallet on Metal testnet.
  • Claude plans and writes: it proposes who moves provider, who gets the reserve and which customer to ask, and three Claude models write the paragraphs. Every proposal passes money rules in code before anything moves.

Everything is test money: the Airwallex sandbox, Airwallex's test card and Metal testnet.

Quick start, no keys

You need Node 23.6 or newer and pnpm.

pnpm install
pnpm start          # http://localhost:4378
  1. Open localhost:4378The landing page walks through a recorded live run in five steps.
  2. Open /app and click ReplayPick a recording. The app plays it back from its hash-chained log, after checking the chain.
  3. Click Run simulationThe same engine and money rules on simulated rails, with the rule planner instead of Claude.

Then check the money rules yourself. None of these need keys:

pnpm test            # 108 tests
pnpm check:sim       # 10,000 random runs, about 25 seconds
pnpm check:evidence  # re-checks every recorded live run

A run, step by step

Each step leaves events in the run's log. The figures are from the recorded run in research/live-kit12-run.jsonl.

  1. HoldsEach customer approves a pre-auth hold on the hosted Airwallex card page: $1.00, $0.75 and $0.75. Nothing is charged. A hold nobody approves is cancelled after 15 minutes. Events: hold.placed.
  2. FundMETER deposits part of its 1.50 USDZ float into a fresh clearing wallet per job ($0.25, $0.20, $0.20) and keeps the rest as reserve. Every deposit lands before any job starts, because a deposit only pays from Metal's next epoch. Events: float.deposited, run.funded.
  3. Pay per stepEach step is one paid request over x402: a paragraph costs 1, 2 or 5 cents by model tier, a search 2 and a lookup 3. Claude picks the few paragraphs worth the dearer tier. Events: payment, report.text, step.upgraded.
  4. ShockMidway, one job's report grows fourfold and the cheapest provider slows to 6 seconds. Claude proposes a new plan; code checks it. If a job can only finish by passing its hold and the float can fund it, the customer is asked to raise it; nothing is spent past the old hold until the yes. Events: shock.overrun, plan.revised, hold.raise_requested, hold.raised, job.rerouted.
  5. ReceiptEach card is captured once, for exactly what its wallet paid; the rest of the hold is released. Unused float is withdrawn from clearing, which Metal allows about 20 minutes after a job's last payment. Then the run reconciles. Events: hold.captured, reconcile.*, run.finished.

The money rules

Claude can propose anything. These rules decide what happens, and they are plain functions in packages/policy, checked in order against a running copy of the books so two proposals can't together break one.

RuleWhere
A job's deposits never pass its card hold, so its spend can't either.checkTopUp in money.ts
The reserve never drops below its floor.checkTopUp, checkPlan
A hold is raised, with the customer's yes, before any deposit past the old hold.checkIncrement; order in engine.ts
The capture equals the wallet's own record of payments, once; a mismatch blocks the close for a person.closeAction
The wallet signs nothing unless the offer pays that provider's own key at no more than the quoted price. A provider that tries is blocked for the run.onBeforePaymentCreation in packages/metal/src/job-wallet.ts
No offer above the clearing cap of 5 cents is paid.maxAmount in the clearing client
A customer is asked to raise a hold at most 3 times, and never twice at once.MAX_ASKS in engine.ts
Every dollar of float is in the reserve or in a job's wallet; anything else fails reconciliation.reconcile in engine.ts
To see the rules hold, run pnpm check:sim. It plays 10,000 random scenarios, half planned by a planner that proposes nonsense on purpose, and checks every rule after each one. Remove a rule from packages/policy and it fails within a few runs.

What Claude does

Claude has two jobs and no keys. It never sees card data or signs a payment.

Planning

claude-opus-5-5 reads the whole situation when something changes (a job can't afford its next step, the shock, a customer's answer) and returns typed proposals:

  • reroute: move a job to another provider.
  • grant_reserve: move reserve into a job's wallet.
  • ask_hold_raise: ask a customer for a higher hold, in its own words.
  • pause: stop a job.

Each proposal is shown in the app with its verdict. Without a Claude key, the rule planner in apps/orchestrator/src/planner.ts takes over and the app says so.

Writing

The three model providers are real models: claude-haiku-5-5 (1¢), claude-sonnet-5-5 (2¢) and claude-opus-5-5 (5¢). Each paragraph is written knowing its position in the report. Search and lookup have no web access; they are labelled as notes.

Live setup

  1. Copy .env.example to .envIt is git-ignored. Never commit keys.
  2. AirwallexSign up at sandbox.airwallex.com and add a business named New Business Sandbox; it is approved automatically and has Payments. Put its Client ID and API key in AWX_CLIENT_ID and AWX_API_KEY.
  3. MetalRun pnpm setup:metal. It creates a testnet operator key, funds it from the public faucet and buys an RPC endpoint over x402, writing METAL_OPERATOR_KEY and METAL_RPC_URL. Safe to run again.
  4. ClaudePut a key in ANTHROPIC_API_KEY (and ANTHROPIC_WORKSPACE_ID only if the key isn't scoped to a workspace).
  5. Runpnpm start, open /app, click Run live. Each customer approves their hold at /approve?job=A (and B, C) with the test card 4035 5010 0000 0008, any future expiry and CVC 123.

A full live run takes about 11 minutes; one payment takes about 7 seconds on testnet. Keep the machine awake during a run. /api/run?mode=live&jobs=1 runs a single short job in about a minute, for checking the rails.

Drills and recovery

The app's Break it panel works during a live or simulated run.

Overrun + slowdownFires the Kit 12 shock now.every Kit 12 run
Fail after paymentThe next request is paid, then fails. It still costs its price; METER tries once more.a real one in live-kit12-no-raise-run.jsonl
Lose an answerThe next paid request's connection drops before the answer. METER books it as paid and buys the step again.live-lost-answer-drill.jsonl
Reprice over capA provider asks more than METER will pay. Nothing is paid.metal-clearing-probe.json
Swap the payeeThe next provider asked names another key as payee. Nothing is signed; it is blocked; the step is bought elsewhere.live-swapped-payee-drill.jsonl

If the server dies mid-run

Settle the run from its log and the wallets' own records. It captures what each wallet paid and cancels the rest. Running it twice changes nothing.

pnpm recover runs/<run id>.sqlite

HTTP API

The server listens on 127.0.0.1:4378. The app uses these; you can too.

RequestDoes
POST /api/run?mode=sim|live|replayStarts a run. recording= picks a replay; jobs=1 runs one short job; shock=off skips the shock.
GET /api/stateThe current run: jobs, float, market, waiting holds and raises, result.
GET /api/events?after=nThe run's log as server-sent events, from event n, then live.
GET /api/recordings/:nameA recorded run's log as JSON lines: kit12, kit12-no-raise, swapped-payee, lost-answer, crash-recovery.
GET /api/holds/:jobA hold waiting for its customer, for the approval page.
POST /api/holds/:job/confirmedThe approval page reports a confirmed hold; the server re-reads it from Airwallex before trusting it.
POST /api/raises/:jobThe customer's answer: {"approve": true, "newTotal": "$0.90"}. The total must match what they were shown.
POST /api/drills/:nameshock, fail-after-payment, lose-answer, reprice-over-cap, swap-payee.

Known limits

  • The test customers are played by us: the holds use Airwallex's test card, and the raise in the main run was approved by clicking the dashboard's button.
  • Search and lookup have no web access. They are labelled as notes and never present anything as a retrieved result.
  • Whether a customer is asked to raise depends on the money at that moment: in one recorded run it was asked, in another it could not be funded and was not.
  • Metal's testnet has slow stretches. One run met repeated deposit timeouts and a deposit of unknown outcome; it did not balance and said so, charged every customer exactly what was paid, and stopped the largest job short. The wallet now looks such a deposit up and settles it; that fix is unit-tested but has not met the same failure live.
  • The readout of when unused float becomes claimable uses Metal's contextOf call and has not been seen live yet.
  • An approval page left open about an hour can fail with "Authentication failed". Recovery settles a crashed run; it doesn't resume it.
