1. A hold, not a prepayment

The customer approves a hold on their card for the job's budget. Nothing is charged yet, and the job can never cost more than the hold.

When things go wrong

The agent plans.
Code holds the money rules.

Claude decides who moves provider, who gets the reserve and which customer to ask. It holds no keys, and every proposal is checked in code before anything moves. These are from recorded live runs.
