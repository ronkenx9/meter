Who it's for

Anyone who sells AI work
by the job

  • Research and document services

    You buy from several model and data providers and have to quote a job before you know what it will cost.

  • Agent platforms

    Your customers start long tasks and need a spending ceiling they can trust before they press go.

  • Finance teams at AI companies

    Today you absorb the overruns or surprise the customer with them. A hold and a raise request replace both.
